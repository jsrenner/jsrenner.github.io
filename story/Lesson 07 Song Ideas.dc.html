LESSON 07 · SONGWRITING COMPANION

Abraham: A Promise to a Nobody

Nine directions. This is the most singable lesson so far — promise before performance, trust without certainty, blessing that has to keep moving — and the one place in the course where the curriculum's own recurring line can become an actual chorus.

SOURCE · LESSON 07 · CALL, COVENANT, ELECTION, GENESIS 22

THEOLOGICAL CENTER
The promise comes before the performance
EMOTIONAL CENTER
Walking without a map, and staying
AVOID
Abraham as flawless faith hero
KEEP AS RECURRING COURSE LANGUAGE
Babel says: “Let us make a name.”
God says to Abraham: “I will make your name great.”
SONG IDEA 01
A Promise to a Nobody
CINEMATIC FOLK · HUMBLE
CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
God's answer to Babel begins with an ordinary person, not a king or an empire.
HOOK
You didn't choose a king
You didn't raise an army
You whispered to a nobody
“Leave and walk with Me”
CHORUS
A promise to a nobody
A road without a map
A future he could never hold
A word he had to trust
VERSES
Set it directly against Babel's scale — towers, cities, names in stone — and then drop to one tent. The smallness is the argument.
KEY IMAGERY
Stars · road · tent · desert · open horizon.
SCRIPTURE
Genesis 12:1–3 · Genesis 15
SONG IDEA 02 · BABEL ↔ ABRAHAM
Make Your Name / Receive Your Name
REFLECTIVE · ACCESSIBLE
CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
Babel says “let us make a name for ourselves.” God tells Abraham, “I will make your name great.”
HOOK
I spent my life making a name
Building something high enough to stay
Then You called me out into nowhere
And said, “Let Me give it away”
CHORUS
I don't have to make my name
I don't have to build that high
If the One who knows the stars
Already knows mine
VERSES
The most immediately modern song on the sheet: achievement, platform, career, status, and religious performance are all Babel in miniature. Name one of those per verse without preaching about it.
SCRIPTURE
Genesis 11:4 · Genesis 12:2 · Isaiah 43:1
SONG IDEA 03 · THE CURRICULUM'S OWN THEOLOGY, SUNG
Already Promised
WARM · MEMORABLE
CONGREGATIONAL · RECURRING
CORE IDEA
Promise precedes performance — a direct extension of the recurring line: I don't have to do to get. I already have it, so I get to do.
HOOK
Before I walked a mile
Before I proved a thing
Before I knew where I was going
You had already promised me
PRE-CHORUS
I don't walk to earn the promise
I walk because You spoke
I don't obey to make You faithful
I move because I know
CHORUS
I don't have to do to get
I already have it, so I get to
Love because I'm loved
Give because You gave
Walk because the promise
Was already on the way
VERSES
This one could recur across the whole course and land again with Paul. The theological guardrail: obedience is never cancelled, only reordered — Abraham does leave, does trust, does obey. Keep the verbs active so grace does not read as passivity.
SCRIPTURE
Genesis 12 · Genesis 15 · Romans 4 · Galatians 3
SONG IDEA 04 · ABRAHAM HIMSELF
Trust Enough to Walk
INTIMATE · ACOUSTIC
CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
Faith is not perfect certainty. It is trust sufficient for the next step.
REFRAIN
I don't need to see the ending
I don't need to know it all
Give me trust enough to listen
Give me trust enough to walk
VERSE
Some days I believed the promise
Some days I tried to make it true
Three steps into trusting
Two steps back to what I knew
VERSES
That second verse quietly carries the course's whole developmental frame — three steps forward, two steps back — inside an ordinary confession. Do not annotate it; let it just be true.
SCRIPTURE
Hebrews 11:8 · Genesis 12–21
SONG IDEA 05 · ELECTION AS VOCATION
Blessed to Become a Blessing
UPLIFTING · SENDING SONG
CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
Election is vocation, not superiority. The narrowing was always for the widening.
HOOK
If blessing stops with me
Then I have missed the blessing
If grace becomes a wall
Then I have missed Your heart
CHORUS
Blessed to become a blessing
Chosen so the world can know
Every gift You put into my hands
Was always meant to flow
VERSES
The risk is generic worship-pop. The fix is specificity: name what a blessing that stops actually looks like — a closed table, a guarded border, a church that has become a wall. Returns later at Israel, church, mission, exclusion and tribalism.
SCRIPTURE
Genesis 12:2–3 · Galatians 3:8
SONG IDEA 06 · SHORT REFRAIN RATHER THAN FULL SONG
Chosen For, Not Chosen Over
DECLARATIVE · HYMN-LIKE
CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
Election as responsibility, compressed to its shortest possible form.
HOOK
Chosen for
Not chosen over
Called to carry
Not to own
Given grace
To give it further
Blessed to lead the blessing home
USE
Better as a sung refrain than a full song — a benediction at the end of a gathering, or a tag repeated after another song. Two chords, no bridge, easy enough to be sung from memory by the second week.
SCRIPTURE
Genesis 12:3 · Galatians 3:8
SONG IDEA 07 · DEVELOPMENTAL REVELATION
What Did Abraham Hear?
THOUGHTFUL · MYSTERIOUS
NOT CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
Revelation comes through cultural categories. Abraham can encounter God genuinely without understanding God perfectly.
HOOK
You spoke into a world of altars
He heard You through the words he knew
How much was ancient fear speaking?
How much was really You?
THE TURN
Maybe revelation comes like sunrise
Not everything is bright at once
Enough light for the next step
More light still to come
VERSES
Unusual and very aligned with the curriculum's theology. Keep it genuinely questioning rather than skeptical — the song believes God spoke; it is unsure how much of what was heard was hearing.
KEY IMAGERY
Dawn · partial light · altar · knife · ram · a voice.
SONG IDEA 08 · GENESIS 22 · THE SACRIFICE THREAD
The Ram in the Thicket
HAUNTING · UNRESOLVED
NOT CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
Genesis 22 read through the sacrifice-development lens — the knife stopped, provision given, the question left open.
REFRAIN
There was a ram in the thicket
There was another way
The knife did not get the final word
The child walked home that day
THE TURN
Maybe we thought gods demanded
Everything we loved
Maybe slowly You were teaching
What You really wanted from us
DISCIPLINE
Keep every claim in the conditional. The lyric must stay exploratory rather than announcing that Genesis 22 definitively means God was abolishing child sacrifice — “maybe” is load-bearing, not hedging. The song's job is the unresolved feeling, which is also the lesson's.
SCRIPTURE
Genesis 22 · Hosea 6:6 · Matthew 9:13
SONG IDEA 09
The Promise Survives My Fear
CONFESSIONAL · HUMAN
CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
Abraham's mistakes do not destroy the promise — which keeps him from becoming a flawless faith hero.
HOOK
I lied when I was frightened
I rushed when You said wait
I tried to manufacture
What You said You'd give by grace
CHORUS
Somehow the promise survives my fear
Somehow Your faithfulness stays near
I keep trying to hold tomorrow
You keep teaching me to trust You here
VERSES
Egypt and Hagar are the specifics underneath this. Worth being honest that the rushing costs someone else — a song about surviving your own failure should not quietly erase the person who paid for it.
SCRIPTURE
Genesis 12:10–20 · Genesis 16 · Genesis 21:1–7

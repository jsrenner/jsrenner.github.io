HISTORICAL FACT

Two ways to begin a world

ENUMA ELISH · BABYLON
Babylon's creation poem, c. 1100 BC — recited at the New Year festival
  • Creation out of divine warfare
  • The world built from a body
  • Humans made to labor for gods
  • Order won by violence
GENESIS · ISRAEL
Israel's creation account, written c. 900–500 BC
  • Creation by speech
  • The world called good
  • Humans bear the divine image
  • Order given as blessing

THE TENSION

If creation is good, where does everything that isn't good come from?

TRADITIONAL VIEW

A good creation was damaged by a real human refusal. Evil is a corruption of something whole — not a rival substance.

ALTERNATIVE READING

Genesis describes a world still unfinished — freedom and risk built in. Suffering is the cost of a creation able to become something.

WE ARE NOT RESOLVING THIS TODAY. HOLD IT UNTIL LESSON 05.
SPECULATIVE METAPHYSICAL POSSIBILITY

Think Deeper: creation as God's first incarnation

If God is self-giving by nature, creation itself may be a kind of self-expression in matter. Bethlehem would then be an intensification — not a first appearance.

Not a defined doctrine. Not a scientific claim. A proposal that makes the real question sharper: is matter something God tolerates, or something God has always been willing to be found inside?

THE CHRISTIAN STORY · LESSON 03 LEADER NOTES · 60 MIN
LEADER NOTES

Genesis: Creation Is Gift

THE ONE THING

If they leave remembering only one sentence: the Bible does not begin with a rule or a fight. It begins with a gift.

RUN OF SHOW

MIN SEGMENT WHAT YOU DO
0–5OpenAsk: “What is the first thing you were ever given that you did not earn?” Two or three answers, no commentary.
5–15The worldSketch Enuma Elish briefly: the world made from a slain body, humans made to do the gods' labor. Do not mock it — take it seriously as a real answer to a real question.
15–30The textRead Genesis 1 aloud, whole. Then the three words: tov, bara, tohu va-vohu. Keep it to ten minutes.
30–45ExercisePairs write the six-sentence “fight” creation story, then name the divergences. Ten minutes writing, five reporting.
45–58DiscussionQuestions 01 and 05 are the load-bearing ones. Skip the rest if time is short.
58–60CloseRestate the one thing. Give the take-home practice. Do not resolve the tension — it belongs to Lesson 05.

WHERE IT USUALLY GOES SIDEWAYS

“So is it six literal days?”

Name the question as real, then redirect: Genesis is answering whose world this is and what kind of world it is. Say you will not settle the mechanism tonight, and mean it.

The room drifts into science

One sentence of acknowledgment, then back to the text. Offer the Think Deeper section as an after-class conversation.

Someone cannot hear “good”

If a person's life makes “creation is good” sound false, do not argue. Say the tension is named in this lesson on purpose and is held, not answered, until Lesson 05.

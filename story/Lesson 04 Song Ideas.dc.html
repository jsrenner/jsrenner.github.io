LESSON 04 · SONGWRITING COMPANION

Christ Before Jesus: Logos and Creation

Seven directions. Christ belonging to the beginning rather than arriving late; John placing him inside Genesis; several civilizations leaning unknowingly toward one moment; truth scattered as seed before anyone knew the name; and the image of the invisible God as the test of every god we have drawn.

SOURCE · LESSON 04 · LOGOS, WISDOM, FULLNESS OF TIME, THE IMAGE

SONG IDEA 01 · THE LESSON'S CENTRAL CLAIM
Christ Is Not Plan B
CINEMATIC · DECLARATIVE
CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
Jesus is not God's late response to human failure. The Logos belongs to creation from the beginning.
HOOK / REFRAIN
Christ is not Plan B
Not a rescue written late
Before the wound, before the fall
You were there beneath it all
VERSES
Open inside the framework everyone already carries — creation, fall, then Jesus arrives. Then reverse it line by line: before sin, before Abraham, before law, before Bethlehem, the Logos already was. From there move into John 1 and Colossians 1 and let the scale open out.
KEY IMAGERY
Beginning · light · stars · breath · Word · creation · Bethlehem · cross.
SCRIPTURE
John 1:1–5 · Colossians 1:15–20 · 1 Corinthians 8:6 · Hebrews 1:1–3
SONG IDEA 02
In the Beginning Was You
WONDER-FILLED · INTIMATE
CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
John takes the language of Genesis and places Christ inside the beginning.
HOOK / REFRAIN
In the beginning was You
Before the sky had learned its blue
Before the earth had found its place
Everything began in grace
ALTERNATE
In the beginning was the Word
Before a human voice was heard
Before we knew Your human name
You were there before we came
VERSES
Creation imagery moving steadily toward incarnation: Word → creation → light → history → flesh. Land the final verse on the turn — the hands that formed the stars became hands we could touch.
KEY IMAGERY
Light before sunrise · Word before language · hands that made stars · flesh.
SCRIPTURE
Genesis 1:1–5 · John 1:1–18
SONG IDEA 03 · THE LESSON'S DISTINCTIVE IDEA
At the Intersection
HISTORICAL · DOCUMENTARY
NOT CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
The fullness of time: multiple streams of human history seem to converge around the arrival of Jesus.
HOOK / REFRAIN
Israel carried the story
Greece was searching for the name
Roads were crossing through an empire
Then into history You came
CHORUS
At the intersection
Of promise, word and time
What looked like separate rivers
Met in one life
VERSES
One verse per stream. 1 — Israel: covenant, prophets, Wisdom, Messiah. 2 — Greece: Logos, reason, the question of truth. 3 — Alexandria: Scripture translated into Greek, Philo at his desk. 4 — the first-century world, where the streams converge. Almost nobody writes songs about historical preparation for the incarnation; that is the reason to write this one.
KEY IMAGERY
Rivers converging · roads · scrolls · languages · empires · crossroads.
SCRIPTURE
Galatians 4:4 · Acts 17:22–28 · John 1:1
SONG IDEA 04 · THE QUESTIONING REGISTER
The World Was Becoming Ready
CONTEMPLATIVE · MYSTERIOUS
NOT CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
What if the fullness of time included humanity developing enough conceptual, linguistic and spiritual capacity for Christ to be received and the message to spread?
REFRAIN
While one people kept the promise
Another searched for truth
While kingdoms rose and languages crossed
The world was becoming ready for You
MORE SUBTLE
We thought the stories were separate
We couldn't see what they would become
A thousand roads were moving
Toward the fullness of time
VERSES
Keep it deliberately speculative. It should feel like wonder rather than certainty — someone asking, never someone proving.
KEY IMAGERY
Seeds underground · roads being built · languages crossing · dawn approaching.
SCRIPTURE
Galatians 4:4 · Ecclesiastes 3:1 · Acts 17
SONG IDEA 05
Seeds of the Logos
WARM · EXPANSIVE
CONGREGATIONAL WITH CARE
CORE IDEA
Justin Martyr's idea that genuine truth outside Christianity can still belong to Christ, because the Logos has been at work everywhere.
HOOK
Every truth is already Yours
Every light comes from the Light
Seeds were scattered long before
We learned to call Your name
CHORUS
Seeds of the Logos
Hidden in the ground
Truth was growing everywhere
Before the church came around
Every glimpse of beauty
Every honest search for true
If the Logos fills creation
Then the truth belongs to You
VERSES
Philosophers, poets, seekers, Israel, outsiders — different people touching fragments. Then the contrast that keeps this from collapsing into religious equivalence: they may find pieces of truth; Christianity claims Truth became flesh.
KEY IMAGERY
Seeds · light · fragments · mirrors · sparks · harvest.
SCRIPTURE
John 1:9 · Acts 17:23–28 · Justin Martyr, First Apology
SONG IDEA 06 · BRIDGES LESSONS 02 & 04 · POSSIBLE COURSE THEME
The Image of the Invisible
PRAYERFUL · INTROSPECTIVE
CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
If humanity is made in God's image and Christ is called the image of the invisible God, then Christ reveals what humanity — and God — are actually meant to look like.
HOOK
Image of the invisible
Show us what we couldn't see
If I want to know the Father
Let me look at who You are
THE PROJECTION TURN
Break every god I made from fear
Every face that looks like me
Image of the invisible
Teach my eyes what God can be
VERSES
Move straight out of Lesson 02's projection theme into Lesson 04's Christ-centered answer: we make God in our image; Christ becomes the corrective image. This one could carry the whole curriculum, not merely Lesson 04.
KEY IMAGERY
Mirror · face · image · eyes · light · distorted reflection.
SCRIPTURE
Colossians 1:15 · John 1:18 · Hebrews 1:3 · John 14:9
SONG IDEA 07 · THE CONFESSIONAL REGISTER
Place It Beside Jesus
CONFESSIONAL · PROPHETIC
CONGREGATIONAL
CORE IDEA
Christ becomes the measure by which Christians test their images of God. The test is first for ours, never a weapon for other people's.
HOOK
Take every picture I have drawn
Every god I've justified
Place it beside Jesus
Let the false ones fall aside
CHORUS
Place it beside Jesus
Put the image in the light
Does it look like wounded mercy?
Does it love its enemies tonight?
If the face I've called the Father
Doesn't look like what You do
Place it beside Jesus
And teach me what is true
VERSES
Each verse names one inherited image and sets Christ's action beside it — the warrior god, the tribal god, the punishing god, the controlling god. Keep every second line concrete and physical; never let the correction become an argument.
KEY IMAGERY
Two portraits side by side · mirror · light · face · cross.
SCRIPTURE
Colossians 1:15 · Hebrews 1:3 · John 14:9 · Luke 23:34

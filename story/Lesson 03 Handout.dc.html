LESSON 03 · CREATION & GIFT

Genesis: Creation Is Gift

Before law, before sin, before sacrifice, before religion — there is gift. What changes when the story starts with generosity instead of a rule?

BIBLICAL
Genesis 1–2
WHEN
c. 1200–500 BC
WHERE
Babylon · Canaan · Judah
SHIFT
Fear → Gift

01 · WORDS THAT MATTER

טוֹב
TOV · GOOD
Not moral perfection — fitting, working, as it should be. Closer to “fit for purpose” than to “sinless.” Seven times in Genesis 1, then very tov.
בָּרָא
BARA · CREATE
A verb Scripture uses only with God as its subject. Never used of human making — whatever it means, it is not carpentry.
תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ
TOHU VA-VOHU
Formless and empty — not evil but unshaped potential. God's first move is not to destroy it but to give it form, light and boundary.

02 · DISCUSSION

01If the world began as a gift, what does that change about how you think God feels about you before you have done anything?
02Israel's neighbors said the world was built out of a fight. Which of those two stories does the world actually look like to you?
03Genesis calls creation good. Is that a claim about the world, or a claim about God?
04What is the difference between believing God made you and trusting that God is for you?
05Where in your own life do you act as if there is not enough?

03 · GROUP EXERCISE

In pairs, write a six-sentence creation story in which the world begins from a fight. Then read Genesis 1 aloud and name every place the two stories diverge.

YOUR SIX SENTENCES

04 · TAKE IT HOME

Read Genesis 1–2 slowly this week, once. Each day, write one line naming something you received rather than earned.
